STEADY-STATE WEAR VS UNMODIFIED ZEPHYR NVS v4.4.2
Same NOR geometry: 2x4096 physical pages, W=4, CRC32, no persistent lookup cache.
Eight 4-byte keys; 2000 changed warmup updates, then 20000 measured updates.
Both round-robin-eight and one-hot-seven-cold workloads; final values verified.
Counts exclude initialization, seeding and warmup:
  ZNVS: GC interval=196 updates, 102 erases [51,51], 417544 program bytes, 82958 write callbacks.
  Zephyr: GC interval=247 updates, 81 erases [41,40], 330368 program bytes, 61296 write callbacks.
  ZNVS: 20.8772 programmed bytes/update; Zephyr: 16.5184 programmed bytes/update.
  ZNVS still has about 26% higher erase frequency in this workload.
Source: tests/bench_wear.c; raw callback statistics in final-matrix/wear.log.
